Experiment Setup

4 — Write Stalls Are Severe

Mean stall: 34.3 %, max: 71.8 %. 14 of 58 runs spent > 50 % of time stalled.
Paradox: small wbuf (4 MB) has highest stall % but also highest throughput — frequent small flushes beat infrequent large ones under memory pressure.
NebulaStream impact: stalls back-pressure the entire stream pipeline, causing cascading delays.
