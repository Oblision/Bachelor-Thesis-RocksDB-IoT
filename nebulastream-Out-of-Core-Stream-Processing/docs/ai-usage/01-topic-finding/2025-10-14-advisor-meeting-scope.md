## Purpose

Working out scope options and questions to bring to the first advisor meeting, on a topic taken from the DIMA topic list.

## Outcome

The topic came from the DIMA topic list; it was not proposed by an AI tool. The
scope was settled with the advisor in the meeting this conversation prepared
for. The model listed both RocksDB and LMDB as candidate backends and the author
was initially inclined towards LMDB; the advisor steered the choice to RocksDB,
which is the backend the thesis implements. LMDB does not appear in the thesis.
The model's other concrete proposals — a spill-policy naming, a 48-run
experiment matrix, and a >=20 % tail-latency success criterion — were not
adopted either.
