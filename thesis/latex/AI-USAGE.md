## What the records are

**Phases 1-3** preceded this thesis as part of the accepted proposal
(October 2025 to February 2026, ChatGPT). The topic came from the DIMA topic
list. The folders hold browser captures of the conversations. A capture shows
the model's replies in full, but ChatGPT collapses long prompts behind a
"Show more" control, so those prompts are truncated in the capture.

**Phases 4-8** cover the thesis itself (Claude, via Claude Code and the Claude
desktop application). Entries were written up from session logs and desktop
transcripts and follow `_template.md`: frontmatter, goal, outcome, verbatim
prompt, full transcript. Conversations for which no log or transcript survived
are not represented, and where a session's model identifier was not captured
the entry records it as not known rather than reconstructing it.

**Grammarly** was used for spelling, grammar, and phrasing during writing and
revising. It is not a conversational tool and has no prompt or transcript to
archive in the format used here.

## Levels of use

The `level_of_use` field follows the DIMA scale (1 inspiration, 2 supplementary,
3 supportive, 4 content-shaping); the scale and its examples are reproduced in
